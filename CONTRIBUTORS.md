## AI Assistance & Contributor Attribution

AI coding assistants and models such as **Gemini** may be used to help draft code, explore implementations, review diffs, or write documentation, provided the **human contributor reviews, tests, and takes full responsibility for every change**.

* **Commit trailers:** When an AI tool substantially helps author or refine a commit, you may include a trailer in the commit message—for example:

  Co-authored-by: Gemini <gemini@google.com>
